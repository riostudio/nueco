Privacy Policy

This policy explains what MemoPad collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match how the app is actually built, not a generic template.

In short

Your notes and calendar events are encrypted on your device before we ever see them. We store ciphertext we cannot read. Your account display name is not encrypted.
Event times and reminders stay readable on our servers. Only the title, description, and location text are encrypted; scheduling data is not.
Analytics are opt in. Off until you say yes, and note content is never included even when on.
Voice input and AI text tools use OpenAI. Recordings and, if you ask for it, transcribed text are sent to OpenAI to do the work.
Calendar sync only touches calendars you pick. Nothing is read from your device calendar until you turn it on and choose which ones.
You can export or delete everything. Both are available right now in Settings > Privacy & Data.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to the MemoPad mobile app and the backend services it talks to. It covers everyone with a MemoPad account. If you have not created an account, we do not hold any personal data about you.

Where "encrypted on device" applies, and where it does not

Note titles and bodies and event titles/descriptions/locations are encrypted end to end: a key derived from your password (or your recovery code) protects them before they ever reach our servers, and we do not have the means to decrypt them. Your account display name is not encrypted and is readable by us. Event start/end times and reminder settings are not encrypted, since our servers need to read them to send reminders and keep calendar sync working. This does not currently extend to feedback text or a voice transcription you send through the organize or summarize tool, which pass through our servers, and in the case of transcription and text cleanup, OpenAI, as plain text.

Because we can no longer read an encrypted event's title, a reminder notification sent from our servers shows a generic "Event Reminder" instead of the real title; the reminder scheduled directly on your device (set when you save the event) still shows the real title.

4. How we use it

  • To run the app. Storing and syncing your notes and events across your devices, sending reminder notifications, and keeping your account secure.
  • To power features you choose to use. Voice transcription, AI text cleanup, calendar import and sync, and location lookup only run when you trigger them.
  • To improve MemoPad. Only if you opt into analytics, and only using anonymized usage events, never note content.
  • To respond to feedback. Comments you submit may be automatically categorized by AI so real issues get seen faster. This only sorts and prioritizes your feedback for our internal review; it does not decide anything about your account or access, and no human-reviewed decision about you is made solely by this process.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use your notes to train any AI model.

7. How we protect it

  • Notes and calendar events are protected with end to end encryption (AES-256-GCM), with the key derived from your password or recovery code through PBKDF2. We do not hold a copy of your password, so we cannot decrypt this data on your behalf, which is also why a lost password and a lost recovery code together mean this data is unrecoverable. Your account display name is not covered by this and is stored as plaintext.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored or logged in plain text.
  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Attachments are accessed through short-lived, presigned links rather than public URLs.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Export your data. Settings > Privacy & Data > Export my data gives you a complete, readable copy of your notes and events.
  • Delete your account. Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete account permanently erases your account and everything in it. This cannot be undone.
  • Turn analytics on or off. Available any time from the same screen. Turning it off is exactly as easy as turning it on.
  • Turn calendar sync on or off, and choose exactly which device calendars are included, from Calendar Sync in the menu.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to correct inaccurate data, object to certain processing, or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • California residents: we do not sell or share your personal information as defined by the CCPA, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front. You still have the access and deletion rights described above.

10. Children

MemoPad is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.
